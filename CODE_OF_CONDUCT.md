# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making NeighbourLink a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

We expect all contributors, users, maintainers, and community members to act professionally and treat one another with kindness and respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, disability, nationality, religion, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

Positive participation includes:

- Communicating respectfully and constructively
- Listening to different perspectives
- Giving and accepting helpful feedback
- Respecting privacy and personal boundaries
- Taking responsibility for mistakes
- Focusing on what benefits the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Hate speech, trolling, or insulting comments
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances
- Sharing private information without permission
- Spam, malicious content, or disruptive behavior
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or permanently ban users who violate this Code of Conduct. Decisions will be fair, proportionate, and based on the circumstances.
